We keep Postgres and add one read replica

Reads are 92% of queries and peak at 4,800 per second. A replica takes them off the primary for $310 a month; moving to a new database would take a quarter and solve a write problem we do not have.

Add a read replica Chosen

  • Reports and search read from the replica
  • No schema or code change beyond the connection
  • Up to 1 s behind the primary; fine for reports
  • Failover stays on the primary as today
